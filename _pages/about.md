Welcome to my personal homepage. I am **GentleBladesman**, an LLM Researcher focused on advancing large language models through fine-tuning, adaptive learning, contextual reasoning, and efficient architecture design.

Research Focus
======
My research explores novel methods for improving LLMs, including:
- Advanced fine-tuning techniques for domain-specific tasks
- Adaptive learning strategies for dynamic environments
- Optimization for contextual reasoning in multi-task settings
- Simplified, efficient LLM architectures
